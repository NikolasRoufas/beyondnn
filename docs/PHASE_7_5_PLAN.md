## 2. External research questions (fixed)

- **RQ1:** when a mechanism is known to be causal, does BeyondNN distinguish adequate from inadequate evidence?
- **RQ2:** when an explanation identifies the wrong mechanism, does the audit detect a contradiction or insufficient evidence?
- **RQ3:** does audit sensitivity correspond to genuine benchmark ambiguity, or does it produce excessive uncertainty even when the ground truth is clear?
- **RQ4:** can BeyondNN avoid rejecting a known-correct interpretation merely because its control or null is over-restrictive?
- **RQ5:** can BeyondNN identify when an interpretation method is correct for one scope but not another?

The hypotheses operationalising them are EH1–EH6 and KE1–KE3 (frozen policy §9).
